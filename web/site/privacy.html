Privacy policy

Applies to the PocketBooks iOS app and this website. Last updated 13 November 2026.

In short: PocketBooks has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. The developer does not receive your books, your listening history or your server details.

1. Who is responsible

The developer of PocketBooks is responsible for data processing under the GDPR. Contact: henning@mauli.app.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • the address of your server and its API token,
  • the address of the demo library, if you switch to it,
  • your appearance and voice choice,
  • a random device ID, so a server can tell your devices apart,
  • your listening position per book,
  • audiobooks you chose to download for offline listening.

All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Where the app connects

Your own server. PocketBooks is the player for a server you run yourself. The app only talks to the address you entered. It sends your API token with each request and, to sync your position, the random device ID, the book's title and author and your position. This data goes to your server and never to the developer. Connections to a server on your home network or Tailscale network may use plain HTTP, which the app does not encrypt. Use Tailscale or HTTPS if that matters to you.

The demo library. If you choose Try the demo library, the app loads pre-generated audio files, covers and text from www.mauli.app. Like any web server, it can record your IP address, the time and the requested file in its access logs. We do not link this to a person and use it only to operate and secure the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). In demo mode your listening position stays on your device.

The app contacts no other server.

4. What the app does not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, no sale or sharing of data. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location. The local network permission is used only to reach your own server.

5. This website

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other domains. Your language choice is kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the app keeps its data on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time. For anything else, write to henning@mauli.app.

7. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page is updated before the change ships.
