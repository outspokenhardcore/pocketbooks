PocketBooks is an audiobook player for books you generate yourself. Turn the PDFs on your own Mac into spoken audiobooks with the free, open-source PocketBooks server, and start listening on your iPhone or iPad while the rest of the book is still being made.

No account. No cloud. No upload. Your books never leave your own hardware.

TRY IT FIRST
Open Settings and tap "Try the demo library". Five public-domain classics are already generated and play instantly, with no server and no setup: The War of the Worlds, The Time Machine, A Princess of Mars, The Thirty-Nine Steps and The Riddle of the Sands.

LISTEN BEFORE IT IS FINISHED
The server turns a book into speech in chunks and streams it as it goes. Playback begins within a minute, even for a 400-page novel.

READ ALONG
A synchronised transcript highlights the sentence being spoken. Tap any line to jump to it.

REAL CHAPTERS
Chapter marks come from the book's own outline. Jump between chapters, or skip back 15 and forward 30 seconds.

BUILT FOR LISTENING
- Background playback with the screen locked
- Sleep timer
- Your position is remembered for every book
- Six built-in voices
- Download finished books for offline listening
- Light and dark appearance

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR SERVER
Point the app at the Mac running the PocketBooks server on your home network or your Tailscale network, enter the token it prints, and browse your own PDFs. Setup takes a few minutes and is described step by step, including prompts for AI coding assistants, in the open-source project.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party code. The app only talks to the server address you enter, or to the demo library.

PocketBooks is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0. Only use books you are allowed to process.
